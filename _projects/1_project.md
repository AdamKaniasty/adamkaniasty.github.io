**A{P}PI Marketplace** was conceived as an AI agent platform for working with technical and engineering documents. The goal was to help users find relevant information in complex documentation and use it to answer questions with reliable, document-grounded context.

**Advanced and reliable retrieval** was central to the project. I designed and built a knowledge graph-based retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system to parse documents, retrieve relevant information, and evaluate the context passed to AI agents. The aim was to preserve the relationships between pieces of information that matter when interpreting technical material.

As **co-founder and CTO**, I worked on the retrieval architecture and its integration into the application. The system used **LlamaIndex, LangChain, and Pinecone**, with a **FastAPI** backend deployed on **Azure**.
